# NX Porting Kit
<img width="1158" height="487" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1e08b3b5-15c8-41e2-b246-a228a0b1a0d6" />

Native Nintendo Switch ports of Xbox 360 games, in one app. Pick a game, add your own copy, and get an installable NSP.

## Games

- Shank
- Shank 2
- The Bourne Conspiracy (coming soon)

More games appear in the library automatically. Use **Settings > Check for new games**.

## Download

Get **NXPortingKit.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/zhtipu/nx-porting-kit/releases/latest), extract it anywhere and run **NXPortingKit.exe**. Nothing needs installing.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11
- Your own copy of the game (the package file, or the folder it was extracted to)
- Your own Switch `prod.keys`
- About 7 GB of free disk space while it builds
- A Switch that can install NSP files

## How to use

1. Open **Settings** and choose your `prod.keys`. You only do this once.
2. Open the **Library** and click a game.
3. Drop in or browse for your game files.
4. Click **Build NSP** and wait for it to finish.
5. Install the NSP on your Switch. That is all, everything is inside it.

The finished files are saved in `Documents\NX Porting Kit` unless you choose another folder.

## Your game and keys

No games and no Switch keys are included. Your keys are stored in a private folder that only your Windows account can read, and are never shared. Everything is built on your PC.

## Updates

**Settings > Check for updates** installs the latest version of the app.
